<a name="projects"></a>
### 🚀 Flagship & Enterprise Projects

#### 1. 🏥 Enterprise Hospital Management System (HMS / ERP)
* **Domain:** Healthcare Operations & Clinical Informatics
* **Description:** An all-inclusive hospital ERP platform deployed in a high-traffic trauma institute. Centralizes patient registration, OPD/IPD flows, digital prescriptions, lab orders, and department-level clearance.
* **Key Features:**
  - Fast-path triage and patient electronic medical records (EMR).
  - Centralized pathology, radiology, and pharmacy dispatch tracking.
  - Multi-tier role-based access control (RBAC) ensuring HIPAA/GDPR data integrity.
* **Tech Stack:** `PHP`, `MySQL`, `JavaScript / AJAX`, `Bootstrap`, `REST APIs`

#### 2. 💼 Automated Payroll & Human Resource Management System
* **Domain:** Enterprise Financial Automation & Workforce Operations
* **Description:** Enterprise solution automating salary schedules, tax withholdings, attendance synchronization, and department-wise reporting.
* **Key Features:**
  - Automated pay-slip generation with dynamic overtime and deduction rules.
  - Leave management with multi-level supervisor approval hierarchies.
  - High-speed batch processing capable of handling large employee rosters without timeouts.
* **Tech Stack:** `PHP`, `MySQL`, `jQuery`, `Bootstrap`, `FPDF / TCPDF`

#### 3. 🧾 End-to-End Procurement & Inventory Management System
* **Domain:** Supply Chain & Vendor Compliance
* **Description:** Auditable requisition and procurement pipeline designed to eliminate paperwork, prevent unauthorized purchase orders, and track budget utilization.
* **Key Features:**
  - Purchase requisition (PR), quotation comparisons, and Purchase Order (PO) workflows.
  - Real-time inventory deduction, minimum stock alerts, and supplier performance logging.
  - Strict audit trail logs for transparency and compliance.
* **Tech Stack:** `PHP`, `MySQL`, `RESTful Architecture`, `Bootstrap 5`

#### 4. 🧠 AI Virtual Doctor Chatbot (Symptom Diagnostics Engine)
* **Domain:** Artificial Intelligence & Healthcare Assistance
* **Description:** Intelligent conversational AI assistant that interacts with patients, collects primary symptomatology, and recommends possible medical conditions and triage priorities.
* **Key Features:**
  - Natural language input processing matching symptoms against clinical data sets.
  - Interactive questionnaire dynamically adapting questions based on preliminary inputs.
  - Emergency warning alerts advising patients when immediate hospital attention is required.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python`, `Flask`, `NLP / Pandas`, `JavaScript`, `REST API`

#### 5. 🦷 Dental & Vascular Specialized Clinical Platforms
* **Domain:** Specialized Medical Practices & Clinic Automation
* **Description:** Bespoke digital record management and scheduling software built tailored to the exact clinical workflows of dental surgeons and vascular specialists.
* **Key Features:**
  - Graphical tooth chart notation for dental procedures and treatment history.
  - Vascular patient monitoring, diagnostic scans attachment, and vascular health metrics.
  - Automated SMS/Email appointment reminders and digital billing.
* **Tech Stack:** `PHP`, `MySQL`, `HTML5 / CSS3`, `Alibaba Cloud OSS`

#### 6. 🎓 Comprehensive University Academic & Admissions Portal
* **Domain:** Higher Education & Campus ERP
* **Description:** Unified academic management platform catering to thousands of students, lecturers, and department heads across campus.
* **Key Features:**
  - Online student admissions, document verification, and fee voucher verification.
  - Course enrollment, timetable generation, and grading sheets calculation.
  - Departmental noticeboards, student portals, and alumni registry.
* **Tech Stack:** `PHP`, `MySQL`, `jQuery`, `Bootstrap`

#### 7. 💬 Real-Time Collaborative Chat & Communication Suite
* **Domain:** Instant Messaging & Live Communications
* **Description:** High-performance messaging service supporting direct messaging, channel collaboration, file exchanges, and voice/audio session initiation.
* **Key Features:**
  - Instant bi-directional message synchronization with delivery/read receipts.
  - Audio and voice calling capabilities over low-latency socket pipelines.
  - File, image, and media sharing with secure cloud storage integration.
* **Tech Stack:** `JavaScript`, `WebSockets`, `Python / Flask`, `Alibaba Cloud OSS`

#### 8. 🌐 MR Software Official Enterprise Hub ([mr-software.online](https://mr-software.online))
* **Domain:** Digital Agency & Tech Services
* **Description:** The flagship online face of MR Software, showcasing modern web design, speed optimizations, responsive layouts, and live contact points.
* **Key Highlights:** 100/100 Core Web Vitals, SEO optimized, fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and ultra-wide screens.
